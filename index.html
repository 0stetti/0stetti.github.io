About

Keeping transgenes switched on.

I'm a PhD student at the Biopharmaceuticals Laboratory of the Butantan Institute and the University of São Paulo. My research is on ubiquitous chromatin opening elements (UCOEs), short stretches of DNA that protect transgenes from epigenetic silencing. I search the genome for new ones, work out the sequence rules that make them work, and aim to design synthetic versions for producing therapeutic proteins.

My work sits between the bench and the code: molecular biology and genetic engineering on one side, genomics, bioinformatics and AI agents on the other. I also have experience in software engineering, and outside the thesis I build my own tools for molecular biology and bioinformatics.
